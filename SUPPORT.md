# AwardLens Support

Contact [juicernchu@outlook.com](mailto:juicernchu@outlook.com) for AwardLens
support or privacy questions. Include the app version/build and the affected
route or screen. Do not send passwords, private tokens or payment-card details.

The native 1.2 app is free and uses an offline catalog. No account or purchase
is required. A failed data update leaves the last verified catalog usable.
An award reference is not a guarantee of seats or the price shown for a travel
date on an airline's website.

TestFlight builds expire after 90 days. Install the current available beta
through TestFlight; restarting a server cannot restore an expired build.

Open the Settings gear to see the catalog version or choose Check for updates.
There is no Restore Purchases action in the current free app. For historical
account-record or support-email deletion requests, contact the address above.
The [support website](https://akirakusen.github.io/awardlens-support/) and
[privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) provide more detail.
